We read everything sent to us and reply when a reply is useful.

## Report an error or an out-of-date guide

This is the message we most want to receive. Please include:

- the URL of the page
- what is wrong, and what it should say instead
- if possible, a source — a patch note, a screenshot, or footage

Send it to **{{contactEmail}}** with the subject line `Correction`.

## Report a broken page or a technical problem

Include the URL and, if you have it, the browser you were using. Send it to the same address with the subject
line `Bug`.

## Copyright and removal requests

If you own material that appears on this site and you believe its use is not permitted, write to
**{{contactEmail}}** with the subject line `Removal`. Include the URL and a description of the material.
Requests that identify the specific material and the basis for the claim are actioned fastest; we would rather
remove something than argue about it.

## Business enquiries

Advertising, sponsorship, and partnership enquiries go to the same address with the subject line `Business`.
We do not accept paid placements inside guide content, and we do not sell links.
