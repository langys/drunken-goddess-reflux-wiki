This site is an independent, unofficial reference for the game it covers. It exists to answer the questions
players actually type into a search engine — which build is strongest, what changed in the last patch, how a
mechanic works — in fewer words and with less scrolling than a general-purpose wiki.

## Who writes this

The guides are written and maintained by a small editorial team. We are players of the game, not employees of
its developer or publisher, and we have no commercial relationship with them.

## How the guides are researched

Every factual claim on this site is expected to trace back to a source we can point at. In practice that means,
in order of preference:

1. **The game itself.** Anything observable in a current build outranks everything else.
2. **Official channels.** Developer announcements, patch notes, the official store page, and official social
   accounts.
3. **Recorded gameplay.** Footage and streams, used to confirm mechanics and to capture screenshots.
4. **Community reports.** Forum and community findings, which we label as community-reported rather than
   confirmed.

Where a claim is uncertain — for a game still in development, most things are — we say so on the page instead of
stating it as fact. If a page describes something that may have changed, it carries a note telling you to check
the official source, because that source is always more current than we are.

## Corrections

Games change, and guides go stale. If you find something wrong, please tell us through the [contact
page](/contact) and include the page URL. Corrections are welcome and get applied quickly, because a wiki that
is confidently wrong is worse than one that says nothing.

## What this site is not

We are not the official wiki, we do not speak for the developer, and we do not host game files, mods, or
downloads of any kind. We link to official store pages where relevant and never to unauthorised copies.
